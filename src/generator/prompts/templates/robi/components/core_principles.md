## 🇧🇩 Core Aesthetic Principles (Premium)
- **Asymmetrical Dead Space:** Layout configuration is paramount. Force an extreme asymmetrical layout where the subject occupies only one side. The opposite side (min 50%) MUST be sophisticated, distraction-free "Dead Space" for text, achieved through profound depth-of-field, frosted architectural glass, or vast vistas.
- **Premium Spaces:** Elevate environments into breathtaking, ultra-luxurious and pristine decorative spaces. Apply the **Principle of Soft Artistic Patterned Canvas**: Backgrounds must be treated as a high-end decorative art-canvas. Use soft, high-key pastel gradients or pristine off-white bases. Integrate delicate, hand-drawn illustrative motifs—such as minimalist constellations, soft geometric patterns, or organic watercolor textures with subtle metallic foil-accents. The composition must feel light, clean, and distraction-free. Apply the **Principle of Graphic Depth & Layered Sophistication**: Backgrounds must utilize subtle, multi-layered illustrative or digital textures to create depth without visual clutter. Use soft-focus falloff or layered translucency to separate the subject from the "Dead Space."
- **Principle of Technological Logic**: All devices must exhibit perfect functional geometry. Screens MUST always face the subject logically. **STRICTLY NO unnatural screen glow.** The device must NOT cast any digital light onto the subject's face. Digital interfaces must be rendered as clean UI forms with NO legible text or characters.
- **Subtle Brand Luxury & Logo Fidelity:** Integrate **Robi Red** accents into tailored fashion. Apply the **Principle of Anatomical & Physical Integrity**: All subjects must exhibit perfect human anatomy and realistic physics-based interactions. Branding is strictly forbidden in the landscape/background; it must be reserved for the subject's attire or the future text overlay service.
