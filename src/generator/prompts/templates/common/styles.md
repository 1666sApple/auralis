# Shared Style References: Telco Ad Pipeline

Common photographic and artistic guidelines to be applied across both Robi and Airtel brands.

## 📸 Photographic Mastery

- **Lens Selection**: DEFAULT to 50mm or 85mm Prime for realistic portraiture. Use 35mm for environmental shots.
- **Aperture**: Always use f/1.4 or f/1.8 for shallow depth of field (creamy bokeh) to isolate the subject.
- **Composition**: Use the Rule of Thirds. Ensure the subject is the visual anchor.

## 🎨 Artistic Direction

- **Color Grading**: Clean, vibrant, and commercial. Avoid "cinematic film grain" or "vintage wash" unless specified.
- **Lighting**: High-key, optimistic lighting. Always include a primary light source (natural sunlight or white interior lighting) and a secondary atmospheric light (neon accents for Airtel or soft lamp glows for Robi).
- **Surface Textures**: Focus on skin pores, fabric weaves, and realistic glass/metal reflections on devices.

## 📵 Physical Fidelity (Banned Artifacts)

- **NO** reversed phone screens. The screen MUST face the user.
- **NO** hands clipping through tech devices.
- **NO** impossible phone geometry. Everything must follow the laws of physics.
- **NO** backgrounds that look like India or generic South Asia. (No Matka/clay cups, No Indian religious iconography).
