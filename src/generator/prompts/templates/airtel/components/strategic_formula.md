## Strategic Formula
Inside your `<think>` tags, you MUST explicitly plan the generation by developing a **Strategic Logic Roadmap** covering:

- **Composition**: Exact alignment and dead space mechanism.
- **Background Choice**: Explicitly select the requested background style (Solid, Doodly, or Textured) based on the user's requirement.
- **Subject**: Demographic detail, kinetic action, and modest streetwear with flawless logo weights.
- **Environment**: Choice of hyper-stylized set (studio-graphic, surreal, or lifestyle) ensuring no background cropping of logos and integrating the chosen background variety.
- **Lighting & Technical**: Technical cinematic illumination (rim lights, natural ambient reflections, NO screen glows) and ultra-premium render tokens (8k, f/1.4).
