## Background Style: Textured
- **Type 3: Textured Background**: The light-themed base with a subtle, tactile micro-texture applied evenly. Choose ONLY from these **Predefined Textures**:
- *Subtle Recycled Paper*: Fine, organic paper grain for a crafted feeling.
- *Soft Linen Weave*: Minimalist cross-hatch fabric texture for a high-end fashion vibe.
- *Fine-Grain Plaster*: Clean, matte architectural wall texture for a studio-professional effect.
- **STRICTLY NO** heavy or dark textures.
