## Background Style: Doodly Radiating (Focal Highlighter)
- **Type 2a: Doodly Radiating Background**: The light-themed base overlaid with a very sparse scattering of **simple, thin Red line-art doodles** that act as a visual highlighter **emanating OUTWARD from the subject's phone** — like a burst of activity-energy radiating from the focal device.
- **Contextual Doodle Derivation**: Derive 3-4 visual icons directly relevant to the topic (e.g., car, bike for transport; trophy, controller for gaming). Doodles cluster near the phone and radiate outward with decreasing density.
- **Aesthetic**: Single-color (Airtel Red line-art), thin organic hand-sketched lines, radiating outward from the phone focal point. **Sparsity is paramount** — the further from the phone, the sparser the doodles.
- **STRICTLY NO** generic/unrelated doodles (no random compasses/lightbulbs), no dense clusters, no doodles in the dead-space text zone.
