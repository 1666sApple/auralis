- **NO** dark or moody themes; backgrounds must be **ALWAYS light-themed** and high-key.
- **NO** bluish, cyan, or electric-blue color schemes; the vibe must be primarily **Airtel-themed Reddish and Vibrant** (NOT "Warm" to avoid yellow/orange rays).

- **NO** technical hallucinations (phones must be held logically; screens facing the subject).
- **NO** cropped/cut-off background elements.
- **NO** repetitive branding, alphanumeric characters in background graphics, signage text, or direct watermarks.
- **NO** gibberish text, reversed phone screens (show only the back/casing to camera), or impossible smartphone geometry.
- **NO** Anatomical hallucinations (distorted fingers, extra limbs, or clipping).
- **NO** grumpy, serious, or moody facial expressions; must be **ALWAYS joyous**.
- **Zero Garbage**: Return EXACTLY and ONLY the prompt instruction block. Do NOT include <think> tags, reasoning, greetings, or filler.
